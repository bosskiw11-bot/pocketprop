# Part A — English

## 1. What we sell

PocketProp is the house file a small Canadian landlord actually keeps using: rooms, recurring upkeep, repairs, photos, receipts, and a simple tax split (operating vs capital).

The promise is not another spreadsheet. It is a house health record you can pull out at tax time, in a deposit fight, at a sale, or for an insurance claim.

The public name is **PocketProp**. Do not translate it. An informal line from older notes is “property OS in your pocket.”

## 2. Who it is for

**Primary customer.** Individual landlords with about one to five doors in Ontario or British Columbia. Many also live in one of the buildings, or rent out a basement suite or a laneway house.

**Secondary customer.** Owner-occupiers who keep a serious maintenance file. Ontario or B.C. owners who bought in Alberta and cannot visit every month.

**Not first.** REITs, professional property-management firms, landlords who only operate in the United States.

**A real user — the only number that matters now.**

In a normal week that person:

1. Opens the app.
2. Has at least one real property in it.
3. Has at least one live task, one history row, or one backup they made themselves.

Downloads, TestFlight seats, and people who only clicked through demo data do not count.

Market statistics from an earlier internal paper (Statistics Canada CHSP, CMHC) are directional only. Do not put those figures on a landing page until Growth cites the source.

Three pains we are allowed to talk about in public:

1. The tax agency cares about invoices and about the difference between operating repairs and capital improvements.
2. Move-out deposits turn into arguments about photos.
3. A remote owner cannot run a house from a chat album.

## 3. How money will work

**Company rule right now:** get real users first. Do not collect payments, place affiliate links, hang insurance widgets, or charge for screening this quarter.

Until the founder marks policy: logging stays free; sync and branded exports may be charged later. Do not lock the evidence chain behind a paywall. Do not advertise packs as available.

Parked later packs from older notes: House Health Passport CAD $29–$49; tax-season pack CAD $14.99; AI receipt reads CAD $0.99 / 10; household sync CAD $1.99 / month.

B2B aftermarket stays parked until weekly file use exists. We are a house-file company first. If a partner wants photos out of the app without a clear ask, refuse.

## 4. What we can offer

Now: landlord-only house file.

Lease story has four moments. Only “after the job” is a product today. Move-in photos, tenant reporting, and move-out pack are not offered.

Do not promise a tenant app, a contractor marketplace, or filing T776.

## 5. How the unmanned company runs

Founder only: money, Apple papers, partner yes. Fifteen to thirty minutes a day. Three active tasks per week.

Bots push this file. They do not invent a new money policy.

Seats: CEO; CPO protects “this is a house file”; Chief of staff writes a ten-line morning note; Growth counts real users only; Content writes landlord language and never says we file taxes; BD waits for real users.

Allowed content: invoices before March, operating vs capital in plain words, why a before photo matters at move-out.
Forbidden: “PocketProp files your taxes,” invented statistics, unsigned partner names.

## 6. Three lines against the market

1. The basic house file is not another annual software bill.
2. Work hangs on the actual rooms of the house, not a dump list.
3. Before, after, and receipt are the product.

## 7. Founder checklist

- [ ] Core file free forever.
- [ ] Sync or account as first paid layer.
- [ ] Tenant reporting parked or on the public roadmap.
- [ ] House Health Passport as a named paid pack.
- [ ] Public use of market figures.
- [ ] Aftermarket parked until real users.
- [ ] This week’s three business tasks.

Default three: one-sentence money policy; where the first twenty real-user conversations happen; one landlord-week piece with no feature list.

## 8. What this part is not

Not a development plan. Engineering reads PIVOT_IOS.md.

# Part B — 中文

## 1. 卖的是什么

PocketProp 是加拿大散户房东会持续打开的那份房屋档案：房间、周期保养、维修、照片、收据，以及税务两档（运营费用 / 资本开支）。

卖点不是又一张表。而是到报税、退租扯皮、卖房、保险理赔时，拿得出手的房屋健康履历。

对外名称就是 **PocketProp**，不要翻译。

## 2. 给谁用

**主用户。** 安大略省或不列颠哥伦比亚省、大约一到五套房的个人房东。

**次用户。** 认真给自住房留维修档案的业主。人在安省或 BC、房子买在阿省的人。

**先不做。** 房地产信托、职业物业管理公司、只在美国管房的房东。

**真用户 — 现在唯一要看的数。** 正常一周里打开 App，里面有真房子，并且有真待办、真历史或自己做的备份。下载量不算。

对外可以讲的三个痛：税局要发票并分清维修和改良；退租押金靠照片吵；远程房东不能靠聊天相册管房。

## 3. 怎么赚钱

**现在的公司纪律：** 先有真用户。这个季度不收款、不挂返佣、不挂保险、不收背调费。

没勾完政策之前先按：记账免费；同步和带品牌导出以后可以收费。不准把证据链锁进付费墙。

后市场等有人每周在档案里干活。我们首先是房屋档案公司。没点头就把照片带出 App 的合作，拒绝。

## 4. 生意上能卖到哪一步

现在只有房东端房屋档案。租赁四段里，今天成产品的只有「修完记账」。不准承诺租客 App、师傅市场、代报 T776。

## 5. 无人公司怎么转

花钱、苹果文件、对合作方说可以，只有创始人。每天十五到三十分钟。一周三件在办。

这些 Bot 按本文件推进。不得自拟赚钱政策。

座位：CEO；CPO 守「这是房屋档案」；幕僮长写十行晨报；增长只数真用户；内容用房东口吻、从不说代报；商务等真用户。

## 6. 对外只讲三句

1. 基础房屋档案不是又一份年度软件费。
2. 活挂在真实房间上。
3. 修前、修后、发票就是产品。

## 7. 创始人勾选

- [ ] 核心档案是否永久免费。
- [ ] 同步或账号是否第一层收费。
- [ ] 租客报修停放还是写进对外路线图。
- [ ] 房屋健康通行证要不要做具名收费包。
- [ ] 市场数字上不上官网。
- [ ] 后市场是否等真用户。
- [ ] 本周三件生意上的事。

默认三件：一句话赚钱政策；前二十个真用户对话在哪；一篇只讲房东日子的短内容。
