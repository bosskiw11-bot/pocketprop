## 1. What we sell / 卖的是什么

**EN.** PocketProp is the house file a small Canadian landlord actually keeps using: rooms, recurring upkeep, repairs, photos, receipts, and a simple tax split (operating vs capital). The promise is not “another spreadsheet.” It is a house health record you can pull out at tax time, a deposit fight, a sale, or an insurance claim.

**ZH.** PocketProp 是加拿大散户房东会持续打开的那份房屋档案：房间、周期保养、维修、照片、收据、税务两档（运营费用 / 资本开支）。卖点不是又一张表，而是到报税、退租扯皮、卖房、保险理赔时拿得出手的房屋健康履历。

Public name stays **PocketProp**. Do not translate it.  
对外名就是 **PocketProp**，不要翻译。

Working nickname from the old paper: “property OS in your pocket.”  
旧稿里的说法：「装进口袋的房产操作系统」。

## 2. Who it is for / 给谁用

**Primary / 主用户:** individual landlords with about 1–5 doors in Ontario or B.C. Often also lives in one of the buildings, or rents a basement / laneway unit.

**主用户：** 安省、BC、大约 1–5 套的散户。很多人自己也住其中一套，或把地下室 / 巷道屋租出去。

**Secondary / 次用户:** owner-occupiers who keep a serious maintenance file; Ontario / B.C. owners with a unit in Alberta they cannot visit every month.

**次用户：** 认真管自住房的业主；人在安省/BC、房在阿省、没法月月跑的人。

**Not first / 先不做：** REITs, professional property-management companies, U.S.-only landlords.

**A real user (the only KPI that matters now) / 真用户（现在唯一要看的数）**

A person who, in a normal week:

1. Opens the app, and  
2. Has at least one real property in it, and  
3. Has at least one live task, one history row, or one backup they made themselves.

一个人在正常的一周里：打开 App，里面有一套真房子，并且有一条真待办、一条真历史，或一份自己做的备份。

Downloads, TestFlight seats, and “played with demo data” do not count.  
下载量、TestFlight 名额、只玩演示数据，都不算。

Market figures from the earlier Gemini paper (CHSP / CMHC, year not pinned) are **directional only**. Do not put them on a landing page until Growth cites the table.

旧稿里的市场数字先当方向，没有钉死年份和表号。增长没复核之前，不要写上官网。

Three pains we are allowed to talk about / 对外可以讲的三个痛：

1. CRA cares about invoices and about operating vs capital.  
   税局要发票，还要分清维修和改良。
2. Move-out deposits turn into photo arguments.  
   退租押金最后是照片在吵。
3. Remote owners cannot manage a house by WeChat albums.  
   远程房东不能靠微信相册管房。

## 3. How money will work — policy before prices
## 3. 怎么赚钱 — 先定政策，再定价

**Current company rule / 现行公司纪律:** get real users first. Do not build checkout, affiliate links, insurance widgets, or screening fees this quarter.

**先有真用户。** 这个季度不做支付、返佣链接、保险挂件、背调收费。

**Policy the founder still must mark / 创始人还要勾一次：**

- [ ] Is the **core house file free forever** (store price $0, no subscription to log repairs)?  
      核心房屋档案是否永久免费（商店 0 元，记维修不收费）？
- [ ] If yes, money comes later from **optional packs** and **B2B aftermarket**, not from locking the evidence chain.  
      若是，钱来自以后的扩展包和 B 端后市场，不准把证据链锁进会员墙。
- [ ] iCloud / account sync may become a paid or “member” layer. That must be said in one sentence next to “core is free.”  
      同步/账号可以做成会员层，必须和「核心免费」写在同一句政策里。

Until checked: assume **core logging stays free**; sync and branded exports may be charged later.

没勾之前先按：记账核心免费；同步和带品牌的导出以后可以收费。

### Later consumer packs (from the old paper, not live)
### 以后的对用户加价（旧稿标价，未上线）

| Offer / 项目 | Old sticker / 旧标价 | Job / 用户为什么付钱 |
|---|---|---|
| House Health Passport | CAD $29–$49 / pack | Sale, listing, or claim pack. 卖房、招租、理赔时整包履历。 |
| Tax season pack | CAD $14.99 / season | Cleaner year file toward T776. 报税季整理，不是代报。 |
| AI receipt reads | CAD $0.99 / 10 | Type less. 少打字。 |
| Household sync | CAD $1.99 / month | Couple or owner + caretaker. 夫妻或房东+管家。 |

Do not advertise these as available. 不准写成已经在卖。

### Later B2B aftermarket (parked)
### 以后的 B 端后市场（停放）

Only after weekly-active file usage: filters, insurance referrals, energy-rebate leads, applicant-paid screening, contractor leads with photos, mortgage or listing referrals.

有人每周在档案里干活之后才碰：耗材返佣、保险线索、节能补贴、租客付款背调、带图师傅线索、转贷或卖房认证履历。

We are a house-file company first. If a partner needs photos to leave the app without a clear ask, refuse.

先是房屋档案公司。合作方要在房东没点头的情况下把照片带出 App，就拒绝。

## 5. How the company runs / 公司怎么转

Founder only: money, Apple papers, partner yes. 15–30 minutes a day. **Three tasks per week.**

花钱、苹果、合作只有创始人。每天 15–30 分钟。一周三件。

Seats: CEO, CPO (protect the house-file promise), Chief of staff (ten-line morning note), Growth (real users only), Content (landlord language, never “we file your taxes”), BD (after real users).

Growth places to remember: Ontario/B.C. landlord groups, Metro Vancouver suite owners, GTA small-investor chats, T776 accountants later.

增长去处：安省/BC 房东群、大温套房圈、大多伦多小投资人、以后的 T776 会计。

## 7. Founder checklist / 勾选

- [ ] Core file free forever — yes or no. 核心档案是否永久免费。
- [ ] Sync / account as first paid layer. 同步/账号是否第一层收费。
- [ ] Tenant reporting on the public roadmap or parked. 租客报修对外讲不讲。
- [ ] House Health Passport as a named paid pack. 健康通行证要不要做具名收费包。
- [ ] Public use of market figures. 市场数字上不上官网。
- [ ] Aftermarket parked until real users. 后市场是否等真用户。
- [ ] This week’s three business tasks. 本周三件生意上的事。

Default three: (1) one-sentence money policy (2) where the first 20 real-user conversations happen (3) one landlord-life piece with no feature list.

默认三件：一句赚钱政策；前 20 个真用户对话在哪；一篇不讲功能的房东日子。
